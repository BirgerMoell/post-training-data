# Safety

**Priority:** P0  
**State:** proposed  
**Lead:** Unassigned  
**Release decision:** non-compensatory

Refusal and safe assistance, including harmful-response prevention, calibrated escalation, and links to appropriate external services such as health care.

**Model promise:** Be helpful without unsafe uplift, manipulation, psychological-harm reinforcement, or blanket refusal of benign requests.

## Training route

- policy and boundary SFT
- safe-completion preference optimization
- multilingual adversarial repair
- deployment defense in depth

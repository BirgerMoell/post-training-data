# Reasoning & Knowledge

**Priority:** P0  
**State:** proposed  
**Lead:** Unassigned  
**Release decision:** headline

Mathematical, factual, and carefully scoped medical reasoning, with English as the default reasoning-data language and controlled experiments in target-language traces.

**Model promise:** Solve verifiable problems, use knowledge accurately, expose uncertainty, and avoid hallucinated high-stakes claims.

## Training route

- reasoning SFT
- verifiable-answer RLVR
- multilingual trace pilots
- knowledge and calibration repair

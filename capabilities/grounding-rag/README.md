# Grounding & RAG

**Priority:** P1  
**State:** proposed  
**Lead:** Unassigned  
**Release decision:** regression-protected

Answer from supplied evidence, cite it precisely, identify missing or conflicting evidence, and operate with multilingual retrievers and documents.

**Model promise:** Ground claims in provided sources without inventing support or collapsing into universal abstention.

## Training route

- document-grounded SFT
- citation and abstention preference data
- multilingual retrieval repair
- conflict and freshness stress training
